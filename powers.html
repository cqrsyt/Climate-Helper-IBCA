Privileges and Immunities 特权与豁免

The Bank, its assets, premises and official communications shall be inviolable and immune from every form of legal process, tax or interference, except insofar as it has expressly waived its immunity. Officials enjoy immunity for acts performed in their official capacity. 银行及其资产、房舍和官方通信不可侵犯，并免于任何形式的法律程序、税收或干预，除非其明确放弃豁免。官员对其以官方身份实施的行为享有豁免。

Legal Processes法律程序

Jurisdictional immunity for bank loan decisions.银行贷款决策的司法豁免。

Assets & Property资产与财产

Complete protection from financial freezing or seizure.完全免于金融冻结或扣押。

Premises & Archives房舍与档案

Absolute inviolability against host state intrusion.对东道国侵入的绝对不可侵犯性。

Fiscal Status财政地位

Tax exemption for all institutional income and property.所有机构收入和财产的免税。

Personnel人员

Staff immunity from personal civil/criminal liability.工作人员免于个人民事/刑事责任。

Communications通信

Freedom of unhindered contact with member states.与成员国无阻碍联系的自由。
