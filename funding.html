Funding Sources 资金来源

Capital structure designed for sustainability and scale 为可持续性与规模设计的资本结构

1. Member State Capital1. 成员国资本

  • GDP-based paid-in capital基于 GDP 的实缴资本
  • Callable capital (rarely used)可催缴资本（很少使用）

2. Capital Market Borrowing2. 资本市场借款

  • Issue climate bonds发行气候债券
  • Maintain strong credit rating维持强劲信用评级

3. Co-financing Partnerships3. 共同融资伙伴关系

  • Multilateral Development Banks多边开发银行
  • Climate funds气候基金

4. Project Revenue (Revolving)4. 项目收入（循环）

  • Loan repayments贷款偿还
  • Guarantee fees担保费用

The funding model combines traditional paid-in capital from member states with market borrowing capacity and partnerships, creating a revolving and scalable resource base dedicated solely to climate adaptation projects. 该融资模式将成员国传统的实缴资本与市场借款能力和伙伴关系相结合，创建了一个仅专门用于气候适应项目的循环且可扩展的资源基础。
