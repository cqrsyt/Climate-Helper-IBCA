About IBCA 关于 IBCA

Identity and foundational vision of the International Bank of Climate Adaptation 国际气候适应银行的身份与基础愿景

Identity 身份

Full Name:全称： International Bank of Climate Adaptation (IBCA)

Headquarters:总部： Geneva, Switzerland瑞士日内瓦

IBCA is a proposed treaty-based international financial institution established to address the critical gap in climate adaptation finance for the world’s most vulnerable countries. IBCA 是一个拟议的基于条约的国际金融机构，旨在填补全球最脆弱国家气候适应融资的关键缺口。

Why IBCA Exists 为何需要 IBCA

Adverse impacts of climate change — particularly extreme weather events, sea-level rise, and ecosystem degradation — disproportionately threaten Least Developed Countries (LDCs) and Small Island Developing States (SIDS). 气候变化的不利影响——尤其是极端天气事件、海平面上升和生态系统退化——不成比例地威胁着最不发达国家（LDCs）和小岛屿发展中国家（SIDS）。

Existing international financial institutions and climate funds have inadequately addressed the urgent adaptation needs of the most vulnerable. IBCA is designed as a dedicated institution focused exclusively on climate adaptation, founded on sound banking principles and strictly precluded from diversifying its mandate. 现有国际金融机构和气候基金未能充分满足最脆弱国家的紧急适应需求。IBCA 被设计为一个专门机构，仅专注于气候适应，基于稳健的银行原则，并严格禁止扩展其使命范围。

Core Design Principles 核心设计原则

Narrow Mandate狭窄使命

Legally binding exclusive purpose prevents mission creep. Any amendment requires the highest voting threshold. 具有法律约束力的专属宗旨防止使命蔓延。任何修正案都需要最高投票门槛。

Sound Banking稳健银行原则

Operates on sound banking principles while prioritising the most climate-vulnerable nations. 在优先考虑气候最脆弱国家的同时，遵循稳健的银行原则运营。

Win-Win Cooperation共赢合作

Empowers powerful states to support vulnerable states through innovative governance while maintaining robust financial standing. 通过创新治理使强大国家能够支持脆弱国家，同时保持稳健的财务地位。

Complementarity互补性

Designed to cooperate with existing specialised organisations (including WMO and UN agencies) and non-State stakeholders to maximise synergies and avoid duplication. 设计用于与现有专门组织（包括世界气象组织和联合国机构）以及非国家利益攸关方合作，以最大化协同效应并避免重复。
