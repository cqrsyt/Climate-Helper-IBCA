Membership 成员资格

Contributing Members, Recipient Members and Non-State participation 出资成员、受援成员与非国家参与

Two Categories of Membership 两类成员资格

Contributing Members出资成员

Provide funds to the Bank. Capital contribution is based on GDP. Greater contributions mean more votes under the weighted voting system — a standard of financial institutions. 向银行提供资金。资本贡献基于 GDP。贡献越大，在加权投票制度下的投票权越多——这是金融机构的标准做法。

Recipient Members受援成员

Climate-vulnerable countries that benefit from IBCA financing. Priority is given to Least Developed Countries and Small Island Developing States. 受益于 IBCA 融资的气候脆弱国家。优先支持最不发达国家和小岛屿发展中国家。

Membership Criteria 成员标准

  • UN Membership联合国成员资格 — Eligibility is linked to United Nations membership.资格与联合国成员资格挂钩。
  • Capital Donation based on GDP基于 GDP 的资本捐助 (for Contributing Members针对出资成员) — Reflects contribution capacity and determines weighted voting power.反映贡献能力并决定加权投票权。

Differentiated status based on contribution capacity is reflected in weighted voting: greater contributions mean more votes. 基于贡献能力的差异化地位体现在加权投票中：贡献越大，投票权越多。

Non-State Participation 非国家参与

Technical Advisory Panel技术咨询小组

Scientists and experts from non-state institutions provide technical guidance. 来自非国家机构的科学家和专家提供技术指导。

Joint Financing Partnership联合融资伙伴关系

Commercial banks and investors can partner in co-financing arrangements. 商业银行和投资者可参与共同融资安排。

Close Connection with WMO与世界气象组织的紧密联系

Strong institutional link with the World Meteorological Organization for climate data that underpins funding decisions. 与世界气象组织建立强大的机构联系，获取支撑融资决策的气候数据。
