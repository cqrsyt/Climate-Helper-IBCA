Governance Structure 治理结构

Assembly of Parties, Standing Committee, President’s Office and Secretariat 缔约方大会、常务委员会、行长办公室与秘书处

Organisational Structure 组织结构

Assembly of Parties (Plenary)缔约方大会（全体会议）

  • Approve Annual Budgets批准年度预算
  • Decide Fund Allocation决定资金分配
  • Admission / Removal (2/3 majority)接纳 / 除名（2/3多数）
  • Normal decisions: Double-majority voting普通决定：双重多数投票

Standing Committee (Executive)常务委员会（执行）

  • Operational decisions between sessions闭会期间的业务决策
  • Recommend the President推荐行长
  • Supervise the Secretariat监督秘书处
  • Major financial decisions (2/3 majority)重大财务决策（2/3多数）

President’s Office行长办公室

  • Principal Executive Official首席行政官员
  • Public face of the Bank银行的公众形象
  • Final executive decisions最终行政决策

Secretariat秘书处

Handles day-to-day work of the Bank under supervision of the Standing Committee. 在常务委员会监督下处理银行的日常工作。
