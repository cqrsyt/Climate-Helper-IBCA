Exclusive Purpose 专属宗旨

Founded on sound banking principles and strictly precluded from diversifying its mandate. 基于稳健的银行原则，并严格禁止扩展其使命范围。

Resilient Infrastructure韧性基础设施

Financing climate-resilient infrastructure that protects the most vulnerable communities from escalating climate risks. 为保护最脆弱社区免受不断升级的气候风险，资助气候韧性基础设施。

Scientific Research科学研究

Supporting essential research that underpins effective, evidence-based adaptation strategies worldwide. 支持为全球有效、基于证据的适应策略提供基础的关键研究。

Ecosystem Restoration生态系统恢复

Investing in the restoration of ecosystems critical to long-term climate resilience and community livelihoods. 投资于对长期气候韧性和社区生计至关重要的生态系统恢复。

Why a Dedicated Institution? 为什么需要专门机构？

Existing international financial institutions and climate funds have inadequately addressed the urgent adaptation needs of the most vulnerable. Extreme weather, sea-level rise and ecosystem degradation disproportionately threaten Least Developed Countries and Small Island Developing States. 现有国际金融机构和气候基金未能充分满足最脆弱国家的紧急适应需求。极端天气、海平面上升和生态系统退化对最不发达国家和小岛屿发展中国家构成不成比例的威胁。
